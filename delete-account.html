1. Request deletion without the app

You do not need to install Vanoa or sign in to delete your account. Send us an email and we will delete it for you.

Email an account deletion request

Email support@vanoa.app

Subject: Account deletion request
Include the email address of your Vanoa account and say that you want the account and its data deleted. Please send it from that email address so we can confirm the request is yours.

The link opens your email app; you still need to press send. If no email app opens, copy the address into your usual email service.

We delete the account within 30 days of receiving your request and reply to let you know when it is done. If you no longer have access to the account’s email address, say so in your message and we will explain how we can confirm it is your account. Never send passwords, sign-in codes, card details or health information.

2. Delete inside Vanoa

If you still have the app, this is the quickest way. Deletion happens straight away.

  1. Open Vanoa and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to More → Privacy & Data.
  3. Under Delete your account, tap Start deletion.
  4. Enter the code we email you. If you have set up an authenticator app, enter that code too.
  5. Type DELETE and tap Delete my account permanently.

Want a copy first? Use Export your data on the same screen before you delete.

If any part of the deletion fails, your account is kept as it was so you can try again; nothing is left half-deleted. If it keeps failing, email us using the route above.

3. What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your sign-in account, email address, display name, bio and profile photo.
  • Your health and fitness logs: nutrition, meals, recipes, workouts, routines, sleep, steps, water, weight, body measurements, medicines, supplements and fasting.
  • Your goals, settings, programmes and custom foods and exercises.
  • Your food catalogue submissions and the link between them and you.

Cached data and temporary photos on your phone are cleared when you sign out or delete your account.

We do not keep database backups, so deleted data cannot be restored, including by us.

4. What is kept

  • Purchase records: Google Play and our subscription provider, RevenueCat, keep records of your purchases. We keep what we need for subscription management and legal, tax and accounting obligations. These records contain no health data.
  • Shared food catalogue: food information already added to the shared catalogue may remain, without anything that identifies you.
  • Your own copies: exports you saved or shared, and data in Health Connect or other apps, are not on our servers and are not deleted remotely.

For full details, see the Privacy Policy’s retention section.

5. Health Connect is separate

Records in Android Health Connect belong to Health Connect, not to Vanoa. Deleting your Vanoa account deletes the records Vanoa imported into your account, but it does not delete anything in Health Connect or remove Vanoa’s permissions.

  • To stop Vanoa’s access: open Android Settings → Health Connect → App permissions → Vanoa and remove the permissions.
  • To delete Health Connect records: delete them in Health Connect or in the app that created them.

See Health Connect in the Privacy Policy for more.

6. Cancel a subscription separately

Deleting your account or uninstalling Vanoa does not cancel a Vanoa Plus subscription. Google Play handles billing, so cancel there using the Google account that made the purchase:

  1. Open Google Play.
  2. Go to Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  3. Select Vanoa, then Cancel subscription.

You can also use Google Play’s subscription page. You do not need to wait for a subscription to end before deleting your account. Refunds follow Google Play’s refund policy and your consumer rights; see Vanoa Plus terms.

7. If you need help

Email support@vanoa.app if you cannot sign in, a step does not work, or you are not sure your account was deleted. Include your account email and a short description, but never passwords, codes or health details.

See also the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
