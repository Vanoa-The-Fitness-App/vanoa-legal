Introduction

This Privacy Notice for Vanoa ("we," "us," or "our"), describes how and why we might access, collect, store, use, and/or share ("process") your personal information when you use our services ("Services"), including when you:

  • Download and use our mobile application (Vanoa), or any other application of ours that links to this Privacy Notice
  • Use Vanoa. We provide a fitness tracking platform that you can use to log workouts, monitor nutrition, and track your health progress.
  • Contact us for support, or visit vanoa.app

Questions or concerns? Reading this Privacy Notice will help you understand your privacy rights and choices. We are responsible for making decisions about how your personal information is processed. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services. If you still have any questions or concerns, please contact us at support@vanoa.app.

Summary of key points

This summary provides key points from our Privacy Notice, but you can find out more details about any of these topics by using the table of contents below to find the section you are looking for.

What personal information do we process? When you visit, use, or navigate our Services, we may process personal information depending on how you interact with us and the Services, the choices you make, and the products and features you use.

Do we process community food contributions or search quality telemetry? Yes, but strictly on an optional, opt-in basis. If you choose to contribute foods, we collect food information (name, portions, nutrition values) linked to your account, which is de-identified before review. Separately, if you opt in to search telemetry, we collect unmatched search queries and counts to find catalogue gaps. Both are separate scopes, switched off by default, and never required to use Vanoa.

Do we process any sensitive personal information? Some of the information we process — such as your health and fitness data — may be considered sensitive in certain jurisdictions. We process sensitive personal information when necessary with your consent or as otherwise permitted by applicable law.

Do we collect any information from third parties? Only where you choose it: health and fitness records from Health Connect on your phone if you connect it, basic profile details from Google if you sign in with Google, and subscription status from Google Play (through RevenueCat) if you buy Vanoa Plus. See section 1.

Do we use Health Connect? Yes, only if you connect it. We read and write the fitness data types listed in section 1 to keep your Vanoa logs and Health Connect in sync. We never sell Health Connect data or use it for advertising, and our use of it follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Do we show ads or track you? No. Vanoa contains no advertising, no advertising identifiers and no third-party analytics or tracking tools, and we do not sell personal information.

How do we process your information? We process your information to provide, improve, and administer our Services, communicate with you, for security and fraud prevention, and to comply with law. We may also process your information for other purposes with your consent. We process your information only when we have a valid legal reason to do so.

In what situations and with which parties do we share personal information? We may share information in specific situations and with specific third parties, described in the "When and With Whom Do We Share Your Personal Information?" section below.

How do we keep your information safe? We have adequate organisational and technical processes and procedures in place to protect your personal information. However, no electronic transmission over the internet or information storage technology can be guaranteed to be 100% secure, so we cannot promise or guarantee that hackers, cybercriminals, or other unauthorised third parties will not be able to defeat our security and improperly collect, access, steal, or modify your information.

What are your rights? Depending on where you are located geographically, applicable privacy law may mean you have certain rights regarding your personal information.

How do you exercise your rights? The easiest way to exercise your rights is by contacting us at support@vanoa.app. We will consider and act upon any request in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

Application Data. If you use our application(s), we also may collect the following information if you choose to provide us with access or permission:

Barcodes are read on your phone, and the barcode number is then looked up in the Open Food Facts database. Scanner photos are processed and discarded; Vanoa does not store them. Your profile photo is stored with your account until you replace it or delete your account. Profile photos are served from a public web address, so anyone who has that address can view the image; do not use a photo you want to keep private.
  • Approximate Location (optional). If you allow it, Vanoa reads your approximate (coarse) location once to rank regional dishes and nearby foods higher in food search. Precise location and background location are never requested. The coordinate is rounded to about one kilometre and saved with your account, together with a city-level label (for example, "Mumbai, India") produced by your phone's built-in geocoding service. You can enter a city by hand instead, or turn location off in the app at any time. Vanoa never keeps a location history.
  • Notifications, Reminders and Alarms. With your permission, Vanoa shows reminders and sleep alarms. These are scheduled on your phone; we do not use a push-notification service or collect a push token. You can turn them off in the app or in your device settings.

Your device's time zone is sent to our servers so that days and dates are calculated correctly. Some pictures in the app (for example, food and supplement photos) are stock images loaded from Unsplash and Pexels, which receive your IP address when an image loads. When you sign in, sign up or perform certain sensitive account actions, Cloudflare Turnstile receives technical signals such as your IP address and browser characteristics to confirm the request comes from a person, not a bot. We do not use these signals to profile you.

You can change any of these permissions in your device's settings. We do not collect advertising identifiers and do not include third-party analytics or advertising tools in the app.

Health Connect (Android)

In Short: If you connect Health Connect, Vanoa reads and writes the fitness data types listed below to keep your Vanoa logs and Health Connect in sync. Health Connect data is never sold, never used for advertising, and is handled according to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Health Connect is an Android service that stores health and fitness data on your phone and lets you choose which apps can access it. Vanoa connects to it only if you choose to and only for the data types you grant in the Android permission screen. You can grant or revoke each data type at any time in Android Settings → Health Connect → App permissions → Vanoa, or from Vanoa's Health Sync screen.

Data Vanoa reads from Health Connect:

  • Steps
  • Sleep sessions (including sleep stages, when your device records them)
  • Weight
  • Hydration (water intake)
  • Nutrition
  • Exercise sessions
  • Heart rate and respiratory rate (optional). These are read only for the night shown on the Sleep screen, to display overnight vitals. They are processed on your phone and are not uploaded to our servers.

Data Vanoa writes to Health Connect: steps, sleep sessions, weight, hydration, nutrition and exercise sessions that you log in Vanoa, plus active calories burned for workouts you log. This lets other health apps you use see your Vanoa activity.

How we use it: data read from Health Connect is used only to show and track your fitness logs inside Vanoa: daily step, sleep, weight, water, nutrition and workout totals, history, trends and progress. Imported records (except heart rate and respiratory rate) are saved to your Vanoa account, like data you log by hand, so they are available on your other devices. You can delete them in the app or by deleting your account. Disconnecting Health Connect stops future syncing but does not delete records already imported.

Limited Use. Vanoa's use of information received from Health Connect will adhere to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular, we:

  • use Health Connect data only to provide and improve the user-facing fitness-tracking features described above;
  • never sell Health Connect data, and never use or transfer it for advertising, ad targeting, credit-worthiness or lending decisions, or to data brokers or information resellers;
  • transfer it only to our infrastructure provider (Supabase), to provide the features above, or where needed for security or to comply with law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with notice to you;
  • do not allow humans to read it unless you ask us to (for example, in a support request), it is needed for security purposes such as investigating abuse, or it is required by law.

Health Connect data is never sent to our AI provider.

All personal information that you provide to us must be true, complete, and accurate, and you must notify us of any changes to such personal information.

Community Food Catalogue Contributions (Opt-In Scope)

What contribution collects, and on what basis (Clause P1). If you turn on catalogue contributions, the food information you create — its name, portion sizes and nutrition values — is stored and linked to your account so that you can withdraw it and so that it is deleted if you delete your account. The lawful basis is your consent, which you may withdraw at any time.

De-identification before review (Clause P2). Before a contribution is reviewed, it is separated from you. The version a reviewer sees contains the food information only — no account identifier, no name or email, no personal notes, and no date more precise than the month. A reviewer never sees who submitted anything.

Search Quality Telemetry (Opt-In Scope)

Telemetry, as a separate choice (Clause P3). Separately from contributions, you may allow Vanoa to collect the food searches that returned no results, so we can find and fill gaps in the catalogue. This is a separate setting, switched off unless you turn it on, and it is not required in order to contribute foods. What is collected is the search term itself, in a simplified form, together with how many times it occurred. No date, no time, no order and nothing about your account is collected with it, so these records cannot be used to reconstruct what you ate or when you used the app.

Safeguards for search terms (Clause P4). A search term is only stored in readable form once enough separate users have searched for the same thing, so an unusual search made by one person is never stored as text. Below that point we keep a scrambled fingerprint of the term along with a count. That fingerprint protects the term if our database were ever copied by someone outside Vanoa, but it is not anonymous: Vanoa can read it. We describe it this way rather than calling it anonymous because it would not withstand a determined attempt by someone with access to our systems.

5 Do we offer artificial intelligence-based products?

In Short: Vanoa has two optional AI scanners: a food-photo scanner and a supplement-label scanner. The photos you submit are sent to an AI model through OpenRouter, routed only to zero-data-retention endpoints, and are not stored by Vanoa.

What the AI features are. Vanoa uses AI only in two optional camera features:

  • Food-photo scanner: estimates what a photographed meal contains and its approximate calories and nutrients.
  • Supplement-label scanner: transcribes the facts panel of a supplement label into structured rows.

Barcode scanning is not AI; it looks up the barcode in the Open Food Facts database. Vanoa has no AI chat or AI coach.

How your photo is processed. When you use a scanner, the photo is sent from your phone to our server function, which forwards it to an AI model through OpenRouter (our AI service provider) and returns the result to you. Every request is configured so that OpenRouter routes it only to model endpoints marked "zero data retention", and no fallback to other endpoints is configured. Vanoa does not save the photo in our database or file storage, and does not log its contents. The request sent to OpenRouter contains only the photo and the scanning instructions, not your name, email, account identifier or health logs. We keep a count of scans per account to enforce usage limits.

Your control. The scanners are optional. Results are estimates that you review and can edit before anything is saved to your logs, and they are not medical or dietary advice. If you prefer not to use AI, simply do not use the scanners; you can log food by search, barcode or by hand.

We do not use your photos or data to train AI models.

Specific retention periods. Account, profile and health logs (including records imported from Health Connect) are kept until you delete them or delete your account. Scanner photos are not stored. Approximate location is kept until you turn location off, change it, or delete your account. Purchase records are kept as long as needed to manage your subscription and meet legal, tax and accounting obligations. Surviving Account Deletion: If you delete your account, everything belonging to you is deleted, including any contributions you have submitted and any information linking you to them. Catalogue entries that were created or improved using contributions from several users remain in the catalogue — those entries are Vanoa's own records and contain no information that identifies you or can be traced back to you. Where a catalogue entry drew on your contribution, it is recalculated without it.

11 Controls for Do-Not-Track features

Most web browsers and some mobile operating systems and mobile applications include a Do-Not-Track ("DNT") feature or setting you can activate to signal your privacy preference not to have data about your online browsing activities monitored and collected. At this stage, no uniform technology standard for recognising and implementing DNT signals has been finalised. As such, we do not currently respond to DNT browser signals or any other mechanism that automatically communicates your choice not to be tracked online. If a standard for online tracking is adopted that we must follow in the future, we will inform you about that practice in a revised version of this Privacy Notice.

California law requires us to let you know how we respond to web browser DNT signals. Because there currently is not an industry or legal standard for recognising or honouring DNT signals, we do not respond to them at this time.
