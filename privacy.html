1. Scope and contact

Vanoa is a health and fitness app operated by independent developers, not an incorporated company. In this notice, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to those developers. This notice covers the Vanoa Android app, its related account services, and this website. It does not describe an available iOS release or an active Apple HealthKit connection.

For privacy questions or a request about your information, use support@vanoa.app. This is the contact used in Vanoa’s existing app and website. Do not send passwords, authentication codes or unnecessary health details.

This is a review draft based on the inspected implementation. The responsible controller/contracting-party disclosures, operational request handling and release-specific practices still require approval. Calling the product “Vanoa” does not resolve those requirements.

2. Information we process

Information is associated with your Vanoa account where it is saved to that account. Some features also keep copies or settings on your device. Optional means you can choose not to use the feature; it does not mean information already collected is automatically erased when you stop using it.

Accounts, profile and setup

Account services process your email address, account identifier, authentication credentials, verification and recovery requests, sessions, and any enrolled multi-factor authentication. Google sign-in supplies identity/profile information through the sign-in process; Vanoa does not receive your Google password. Supabase Auth handles credentials rather than storing a readable password in your fitness profile.

Profile and setup information includes your display name, optional bio and profile photo, date of birth, height, weight, formula/sex selection, body measurements, activity and dietary preferences, training choices, sleep schedule, goals, and app settings. Account access and certain setup inputs are needed for the corresponding app experience; a profile photo, bio and individual logging features are optional. Legal-document acceptance, age attestation and optional-feature choices are also recorded. Account-linked scan-usage counters, export-attempt records, recovery/security quotas and subscription event records support access limits, security and service operation.

Health, fitness and derived information

When you enter, save or import records, Vanoa processes nutrition and meal details, energy and nutrients, custom foods and recipes, water intake, steps, sleep timing, duration, notes and stages, weight and body measurements, workouts, exercises, sets, routines, programmes, personal records, fasting, and medicine or supplement names, labels, schedules, taken/skipped records and stock. Records can include dates, times, quantities, units, notes and their source.

Vanoa uses these records and profile inputs to calculate goals, calorie estimates, scores, progress, weight trends and related insights. These are derived health or fitness information, not medical determinations. The underlying records and saved results can be stored in your cloud account and local caches. Some calculations are displayed without being saved.

Photos and scanning

You can choose or capture a profile photo for upload to account storage. The current avatar design uses a public storage bucket and publicly addressable image URL; do not use an avatar for confidential documents or health records. Optional food and label scans send images for processing as explained in AI image scanning. A barcode is read using the camera and its number is used for product lookup.

Optional location

Regional food suggestions can use a one-time, low-accuracy device location reading or a place you type. Vanoa rounds a new location result to two decimal places before reverse-geocoding and saving it with a place label in local preferences and your cloud setup record. The device location service supplies the original reading; a place you type is sent to the platform geocoder. Typed labels are not necessarily coarse: a street address can remain identifying even beside rounded coordinates. This is not continuous location tracking. Android configuration requests coarse, not fine or background, location access.

Your device’s time-zone name is separately sent to the backend for date/time handling, independently of optional location permission.

Location is off by default. You can decline device access, enter a place manually, or leave location disabled. Disabling the feature and erasing an already saved cloud location are distinct; contact us if you need an existing copy removed.

Searches, contributions and quality information

Online food and exercise searches send queries and filters to the backend. Barcode lookups send the barcode number to Open Food Facts. Search terms can reveal health interests even when they do not contain your name.

Food catalogue contributions have a separate, off-by-default choice. If enabled, authored food or recipe names, portions and nutrition values are submitted to improve the shared catalogue. Submissions are account-linked while managed and may be reviewed, corrected or combined into catalogue entries. Removing an account identifier is not a guarantee that content is anonymous.

The app records local food-search/logging quality metrics, including unmatched queries, counts and interaction measurements. A separate quality-upload choice exists, but an active remote uploader has not been established for the inspected app. Turning off quality uploads does not prevent ordinary online searches. Historical backend quality records and their deletion are a separate retention question.

Vanoa Plus and technical information

When the subscription integration is configured, RevenueCat processes your Vanoa account identifier and customer/purchase state, including when no purchase has yet been made. Subscription processing includes products, transaction identifiers, status, purchase or renewal events, expiry, ownership and store environment. Google Play handles checkout payment instruments; Vanoa does not provide its own card-number, CVC or bank-account entry form.

Network services receive request metadata such as IP address, headers and timing. Authentication protection uses Cloudflare Turnstile. Eligible app builds contact Expo for updates. These operational transfers are distinct from advertising. No initialized advertising or third-party crash-analytics integration was identified in the inspected first-party code; this is not an assurance that every provider receives no diagnostics or identifiers.

Reminders, exports and support

Reminders and sleep alarms are scheduled locally. Their content may reveal health or medicine information on your lock screen. You control notifications in Vanoa and your device settings. A remote push-token registration or delivery path has not been established in the inspected app.

Exporting creates a downloadable/shareable copy of selected account data. You can also deliberately share supported workout or achievement summaries with recipients you choose. Files you save or send to another person or app are separate copies. Support requests contain the contact details, messages and attachments you choose to send, processed through the support mailbox and its service provider.

3. Android Health Connect

Health Connect is optional. It is Android’s on-device health data store, not a Vanoa cloud account, Google Fit account, Samsung Health account or Apple Health connection. Other apps may read or write records there under their own permissions.

With the relevant permissions, Vanoa imports records into its fitness features and can write eligible manually logged records back to Health Connect. Imported Vanoa records are not all kept only on your phone: selected records, source identifiers and sleep stages can be saved to your account in Supabase and cached locally.

How synchronization works

The native bridge reads permitted Health Connect records. Vanoa converts values into its logging units, aggregates certain daily values, and uses source record identifiers and saved sync progress to reduce duplicate imports. Initial synchronization looks back over a recent 30-day window; later sync uses saved progress and overlap. That lookback is a sync window, not a 30-day retention period.

Synchronization is implemented around app startup and returning to the foreground, as well as user-triggered sync. The inspected configuration does not request extended-history access, special background health-read access or exercise-route access. Permission history limits and device availability can affect the records returned.

Cloud copies support your logs, history, goals, scores and related fitness features. Supabase processes these copies for Vanoa. The inspected scanner payload does not take Health Connect records or your full health diary as an input to the AI image service. Subscription requests do not intentionally include the health diary. Those bounded findings do not remove the need to control provider logs, staff access or future data paths.

Permissions, cloud copies and your choices

The onboarding connection asks for a smaller read set; the full connection flow can request the read/write set above. The six metric rows in the app reflect permission status; they are not independent read-only and write-only controls. Regular synchronization currently requires all 13 core read/write permissions, even for an import-only or selected-metric run. Denying a core permission blocks regular sync as a whole. Both optional heart-rate and respiratory-rate permissions must be granted for either live overnight summary; they are not required for regular sync. Android Health Connect settings are the authority for the grants you allow. Manual logging and other non-connected features remain available.

Disconnecting turns off Vanoa’s main synchronization connection. It does not erase existing local/cloud imports or records already written into Health Connect. The current separate Sleep-detail reader is not governed by that connection flag, and a sync operation already in progress can finish transferring data. To stop further permitted Health Connect access, revoke Vanoa’s permissions in Android’s Health Connect settings.

Deleting a Vanoa log is not the same as deleting the source record in Health Connect. If a source record remains, a later sync can import it again. Account deletion does not itself erase records written by other apps, remove every Vanoa-authored Health Connect record, or revoke Android permissions. Manage those records and grants in Health Connect and, where relevant, the originating app. Other apps’ copies and your downloaded exports have their own deletion controls.

Restricted use and consent. The intended Health Connect use is the visible health and fitness functionality described here, not advertising, sale, credit or insurance eligibility, or unrelated model training. Actual transfers and human access must satisfy the Health Connect permissions and Limited Use restrictions; merely naming a cloud service provider does not establish that the transfer is permitted. An OS permission alone is not a separate authorization for every cloud or downstream use. The current cloud-transfer basis, prominent upload disclosure, minimum scopes, permitted human access and consent/withdrawal behaviour are unresolved release requirements. Any prohibited processing must be fixed or stopped; disclosure alone cannot authorize it.

4. AI image scanning

Optional scanning estimates meal nutrition and transcribes food or supplement labels. It is not a general AI coach or a service that independently starts workouts or changes your diary.

A selected or captured image is resized into JPEG form and sent to an authenticated Supabase function. That function forwards the image and task instructions through OpenRouter to a model-serving provider. There are two implemented scanner handlers with different model fallback rules; a retried scan can reach more than one qualifying endpoint. A model’s brand/name does not identify the provider that actually served it. An image may incidentally contain a face, handwriting, packaging details, medicine information or other identifying content. Crop out unrelated details before submitting it.

The returned estimates or transcription can become saved Vanoa records when you accept and save them. Check quantities, units, ingredients and label values yourself. You can avoid image processing and enter information manually.

The server requests zero-data-retention provider routing. This is not an end-to-end guarantee of no retention or no training across temporary device files, infrastructure, request metadata, logging and every serving route. The active provider route and its applicable retention/training terms must be confirmed before publication. No universal claim that scanner images are “never stored” is made here.

5. Purposes and choices

Vanoa uses information to provide account access, save and display your fitness history, calculate estimates and goals, operate the optional connections described above, schedule reminders, answer support requests, protect account access, and verify Vanoa Plus entitlements. Optional contributions support catalogue improvement. Online queries and local quality metrics are separate from an unverified remote quality-upload service.

  • Use device permissions for camera, selected photos, location, notifications and Health Connect access.
  • Use Profile → Privacy and Data for the separate contribution and quality-upload choices and account data tools.
  • Choose manual entry instead of image scanning, and manual logging instead of Health Connect.
  • Use app/device notification settings to limit reminders and lock-screen exposure.
  • Request access, correction, withdrawal or deletion using the contact and tools below.

Processing grounds

Optional access and feature choices do not provide blanket permission for unrelated uses. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it; withdrawal does not retrospectively change processing already lawfully carried out. Account/service delivery, security, purchases and any necessary legal records must each have an applicable processing ground. Health information can require an additional sensitive-data condition.

Purpose-specific processing grounds, sensitive-data consent and international-transfer arrangements remain subject to UAE and other applicable law review. This draft does not assume that accepting the Terms or an OS permission satisfies all of those requirements.

6. Providers and recipients

Feature choices and release configuration determine which of these services process information. A provider’s own obligations and retention are distinct from Vanoa’s account-deletion process.

  • Supabase: authentication, account/profile and fitness cloud records, storage, server functions, searches, contributions, scanner requests, export and deletion operations. Authentication emails also involve its configured email-delivery service.
  • Google: optional Google sign-in and Google Play purchase processing. Health Connect’s on-device store is separate from these account/store services.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: browser, device and network signals for bot protection in guarded authentication flows.
  • OpenRouter and its selected model-serving provider: the image/task processing described above, not a verified provider-independent no-retention service.
  • RevenueCat: configured customer identity, purchases and entitlement management, together with Google Play and Vanoa’s verification backend.
  • Open Food Facts: barcode product lookup and associated network request metadata.
  • Unsplash and Pexels image services: requests for stock images used by app content, conveying network metadata and image identifiers.
  • Platform location/geocoding services: optional device location and typed-place resolution before Vanoa stores rounded preferences.
  • Expo/EAS Update: update checks and downloads in eligible builds, including build/runtime and network request information.
  • Hosted authentication fallback: the existing web confirmation route is hosted on Vercel and handles sign-in links and web request metadata.
  • GitHub Pages and its delivery infrastructure: hosting of this legal website and visitor request metadata.
  • Support mailbox services and authorized operators: handling requests you send. The actual mail provider, access arrangements and retention need operational confirmation.

Authored food contributions can be reviewed and incorporated into a shared catalogue. Files or information you deliberately share using export tools go to the recipients you choose. Requests from authorities must be assessed under applicable law and, for restricted health information, the applicable platform restrictions; this is not a general permission to disclose your health history.

Provider contracts, exact automatic SDK fields, human-access restrictions, downstream recipients and permitted-use roles are not fully verified. “Service provider” in ordinary language is not automatically the same as Google Play’s specific sharing exemption.

7. Storage and international processing

Vanoa maintains cloud account records and local device copies. Named Supabase project metadata identifies the primary project region as South Asia (Mumbai), India. This does not establish the destination of every released build or the locations of every authentication, storage, function, log, backup, replica or downstream-provider operation.

Provider processing and operator access can involve countries other than your own. UAE governing law is not a statement that all information is stored in the UAE. The complete country map, applicable transfer safeguards and any health-data-specific hosting restrictions remain unresolved publication requirements; use of the app is not treated as blanket consent to every international transfer.

8. Retention and deletion

Account and fitness records support your saved history while you use the service. Individual-entry and account-deletion tools exist, but account lifetime alone is not a complete retention schedule. See Delete Account for the request routes and their limits.

  • Account-owned records and profile images: targeted by account deletion; completion and full local cleanup still require release verification.
  • Contributions and quality information: account-linked submissions, historical pseudonymous records and promoted catalogue outcomes have different deletion paths. Hashing or removing attribution does not prove irreversible anonymity.
  • Purchase, security and operational records: not all are erased by the same account operation. Any justified retention needs a specific purpose, lawful ground and period.
  • Backups, logs, support messages, caches and provider copies: actual expiry and deletion after a restore have not been established.
  • Device files, exports, Health Connect and other apps: are separate copies; account deletion does not remotely erase every such copy.

Retention schedule not yet approved. Verified category-specific completion periods and expiry criteria are still missing. This draft does not renew a general “within 30 days” deletion promise, an additional 30-day backup promise, or permission to retain information indefinitely. Missing periods and incomplete deletion are publication blockers, not lawful retention exceptions.

Stopping synchronization, revoking permissions, clearing app data, uninstalling, deleting a record, deleting an account and cancelling a subscription have different effects. If a request fails or its result is uncertain, contact support rather than assuming every copy has been removed.

9. Security

The implementation uses authenticated server requests, configured HTTPS endpoints, database access-control mechanisms and protected native authentication-session storage. Ordinary local profile/health caches are distinct from protected session storage. Android configuration disables app backup, but that does not erase server backups or establish the behaviour of every distributed artifact.

No system is perfectly secure. Vanoa does not claim end-to-end encryption, that all local caches are encrypted, that no human can access information, or that a successful request proves permanent erasure. Limit sensitive information in avatars, free-text fields, scans, notifications and support messages. Keep your device and authentication factors secure.

10. Age eligibility

The existing signup policy is for adults aged 18 or over who have the legal capacity to use the service and grant any optional contribution licence. It is not a guardian-consent service for children. An age attestation is not proof of a person’s age. The current Google sign-in path can record one without a fresh age-checkbox action, so the adult-only statement must not be read as verified exclusion of minors. Registration/profile controls and the minor-data response process must be reconciled before publication.

If you believe a child has supplied information to Vanoa, contact support@vanoa.app without sending unnecessary details about the child.

11. Your rights and requests

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to information about processing, access and a copy, correction, erasure, portability, restriction, objection, consent withdrawal and review of certain automated decisions. You can also raise a complaint with the competent data-protection authority. UAE governing law does not remove mandatory rights in another applicable jurisdiction.

Use profile/settings controls to correct supported fields. Profile → Privacy and Data offers an export of selected records; this is not a representation that the ZIP covers every personal-data category. Ask support for data not included in the export.

How to make a request

Email support@vanoa.app with the request and the email associated with your account. We need to verify your identity or authority before disclosing or deleting account information. Do not send your password, sign-in code or authenticator code. An authorized representative may need to show authority to act. The web deletion request route does not require reinstalling Vanoa.

If a request is refused, ask for the reason and, where applicable, appeal using the same contact or complain to your competent authority. Applicable statutory response periods continue to apply; an unapproved operational schedule does not suspend your rights.

Other-market rights

Where applicable, US state rights can include knowing the categories and recipients of information, access, correction, deletion, a portable copy, non-discrimination, and limits or opt-outs for specified sensitive-data processing, sale, sharing, targeted advertising or significant profiling. These labels have legal definitions; ordinary vendor transfers are not automatically outside them. The release territories and statutory applicability must be established before final regional wording is approved.

For the EEA, UK, Switzerland or other jurisdictions whose law applies, the available access, correction, erasure, objection, restriction, portability, withdrawal and complaint rights are not waived by these terms. Contact us for the appropriate request or supervisory-authority route.

12. This website

These pages contain the full documents in static HTML. Reading does not require an account, app installation, JavaScript, analytics consent or an embedded third-party service. Fonts and visual assets are hosted with the site. The candidate pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising pixels or application cookies.

The website host and delivery infrastructure still receive ordinary web requests, including IP addresses and request metadata. Their operational logging is distinct from cookies or tracking code in these pages. Following an external link or opening your email app involves that separate provider. The static email link opens a message; it does not send one, confirm delivery or prove that deletion occurred.

Browser privacy signals

The static pages do not implement a separate response to browser Do Not Track or similar signals. This does not waive any legally required opt-out treatment. Hosting and any future website features must be assessed separately before making a broader no-tracking claim.
