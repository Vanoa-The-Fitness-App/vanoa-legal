Health information and AI

Nutrition values, calories, goals, scores, trends and image results can be incomplete or inaccurate. A scan can misread an ingredient, quantity, unit or supplement label. Review results before saving or relying on them, especially where allergies, medication, supplements or a health condition are involved.

Vanoa’s tracking and estimates are not a substitute for advice, diagnosis or treatment from a qualified health professional. Do not change prescribed treatment or delay seeking medical help because of an app result. Reminders are a convenience, not a guaranteed medication or emergency alert system. For urgent health needs, use appropriate local emergency services rather than Vanoa.

Connected apps and food sources can return different or duplicate records. Review imported history and estimates. The current AI scanning features process submitted images; they are not a general medical chatbot or a promise of individualized clinical care.
