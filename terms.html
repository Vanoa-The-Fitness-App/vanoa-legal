2. The service and its limits

Vanoa helps you track workouts, nutrition, sleep, weight and other everyday health habits, with goals, history, reminders and estimates. Android Health Connect sync and the AI food and label scanners are optional. Vanoa Plus adds the paid features shown in the app. Vanoa is currently available on Android only.

Health information and AI

Vanoa is a fitness tracker, not a medical service. Calories, nutrition values, goals, scores, trends and scan results are estimates and can be wrong. A scan can misread a food, quantity or label, so check results before saving them, especially if you have allergies, take medication or have a health condition.

Nothing in Vanoa is medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Do not change prescribed treatment or delay getting medical help because of something the app shows. Reminders are a convenience and may not always fire. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services.

Data imported from Health Connect or other apps can be incomplete or duplicated. Review your history if something looks wrong.

Your subscription renews automatically at the end of each billing period until you cancel it in Google Play. We use RevenueCat, our subscription provider, to check your subscription status with Google Play and unlock Plus in your account.

How to cancel
